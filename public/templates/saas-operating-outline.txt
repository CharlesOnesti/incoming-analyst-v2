SaaS Operating Outline
======================

1) ARR bridge
   - Beginning ARR
   - New + expansion − contraction − churn = ending ARR
   - NRR / GDR / NDR

2) Unit economics
   - ACV / ASP
   - CAC and payback
   - LTV (or contribution after variable cost)
   - Magic number / burn multiple

3) Go-to-market
   - Pipeline coverage
   - Win rate and cycle time
   - Capacity (AEs, SEs) and ramp

4) Operating plan checkpoints
   - Gross margin and hosting COGS
   - Implementation backlog
   - Support tickets / NPS
   - Rule of 40 path
