Simple Paper LBO Outline
========================

1) Entry
   - Entry EBITDA / revenue
   - Entry multiple → enterprise value
   - Net debt / cash → equity check
   - Sources & uses

2) Debt
   - Opening debt balances by tranche
   - Interest (cash / PIK)
   - Mandatory amortization
   - Optional prepay from excess FCF

3) Operations (hold period)
   - Revenue growth by year
   - EBITDA margin path
   - D&A, capex, NWC
   - Free cash flow bridge

4) Exit
   - Exit EBITDA × exit multiple
   - Net debt at exit → equity value
   - MOIC and IRR
   - Sensitivity: ±1 turn multiple, ±100 bps margin
